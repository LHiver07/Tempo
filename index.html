The idea

What is Tempo?

Tempo is a study planner website designed to help students organize their academic tasks in one place. It brings together a task board, a Pomodoro timer, a calendar, and study statistics so students can plan their work and view their progress.

Why use it?

Having a clear plan can make schoolwork feel more manageable. Students can use Tempo to see what needs attention, set priorities, and build a consistent study routine.

  • Organize tasks by status and track completed work.
  • Use focused study sessions with short breaks.
  • Check deadlines and plan around important dates.
  • Monitor study time and maintain a study streak.
